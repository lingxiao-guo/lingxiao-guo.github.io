Lingxiao Guo

Robotics · Embodied AI

I am a first-year Master student at Robotics Institute, Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Guanya Shi. I received my Bachelor degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (2025). Previously, I was fortunate to be advised by Huazhe Xu at Tsinghua IIIS.

My research interests lie in Robotics and Embodied AI. I am broadly interested in post-training robot foundation models, building next-generation robot data engines that learn from many sources of data, world models, and self-evolved physical agents.
